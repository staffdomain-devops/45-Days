<objective>
Implement `fetch_hubspot.py`: pull all HubSpot data needed to personalise the campaign — contact properties, deal stage, email history (past 12 months), meeting engagements (past 12 months), CRM meeting objects (v4 associations), Chorus conversation IDs (regex from meeting notes), and owner first names.
