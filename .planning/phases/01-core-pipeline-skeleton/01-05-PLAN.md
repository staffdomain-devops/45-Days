<objective>
Implement the final pipeline step. `write_hubspot.py`:
1. Reads `campaign_output.json` from `$RUNNER_TEMP` (WR-01)
2. Writes 7 subjects to `subject_1`..`subject_7` single-line text properties on the contact (WR-02)
3. Writes 7 email bodies to `email_1`..`email_7` multi-line text properties, capped at 65,000 chars each (WR-03)
4. Writes SDR call notes to `eofy26_sdr_notes` and generation date to `eofy26_generated_date` (WR-04)
5. Creates a new HubSpot note engagement on the contact with rich HTML body (WR-05)
6. Logs and continues if note creation fails — does not crash the pipeline (WR-06)

This is the final step. After it succeeds, the sales rep sees a fully personalised 7-email sequence + SDR notes on the contact in HubSpot.

Purpose: Closes the loop. The whole pipeline exists to put content in HubSpot.
